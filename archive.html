Remove yourself from web archives

Archive.org and Archive.Today preserve old versions of your sites — including things you'd rather forget. Here's how to get your own domains removed.

Archive.org (Wayback Machine)
Steps to remove your domain
1. Search your domain at archive.org. 2. Add a robots.txt with User-agent: archive.org_bot / Disallow: /. 3. Add a verify.txt at your root containing "please remove from archive.org". 4. Email info@archive.org from an address @your-domain with "Domain Removal" as subject, referencing both files, plus a DMCA notice. 5. Wait 24–48h, provide domain receipts if challenged (as PDFs, never screenshots).
Search archive.org
Archive.Today (archive.ph)
Steps to remove a capture
Search your domain at archive.ph, open the capture, click "report bug or abuse" (top-right), choose Abuse Type "Copyright", and send a DMCA notice. If ignored, email webmaster@archive.today. They ignore robots.txt — block their IPs at the server instead.
Search archive.ph
Google / Bing cache
Prevent cache copies
Add <meta name="robots" content="noarchive"> to the <head> of every page. Google/Bing will drop cached copies on next index.
